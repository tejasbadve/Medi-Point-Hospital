Heart disease: Symptoms, Causes and Risk Facto

Heart disease, a pervasive and potentially life-threatening condition, remains a leading global health concern. The intricate interplay of genetic predispositions, lifestyle choices, and age-related factors contributes to its prevalence. Recognizing the importance of early detection and prevention, this exploration delves into the comprehensive landscape of heart disease, focusing on symptoms, causes, and risk factors. Unraveling the mysteries surrounding this complex ailment is crucial for fostering public awareness and promoting proactive health measures. Symptoms, Causes and Risk Factor

Symptoms

1.One of the major symptoms of heart disease is chest pain or discomfort, known as angina. The sensation may feel like pressure, squeezing, fullness, or pain in the chest area. The discomfort may extend to other areas such as the arms, neck, jaw, shoulder, or back.

2.Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath can occur during physical exertion or even at rest. This symptom is indicative of potential heart issues affecting the organ's ability to pump blood efficiently. Shortness of breath may be accompanied by fatigue and weakness.

Causes

1.Atherosclerosis is a primary cause of heart disease, characterized by the accumulation of plaque within the arteries. Plaque, composed of cholesterol, fat, calcium, and other substances, narrows and hardens the arteries, impeding blood flow.

2.CAD occurs when the coronary arteries, responsible for supplying oxygen and nutrients to the heart muscle, become narrowed or blocked. This narrowing is often a consequence of atherosclerosis. Reduced blood flow to the heart muscle can lead to chest pain (angina).

Risk Factor

1.Age is a significant non-modifiable risk factor for heart disease. The risk increases with advancing age, and the majority of heart disease cases occur in individuals over the age of 65. Aging can contribute to the gradual accumulation of plaque in the arteries, leading to atherosclerosis.

2.Tobacco smoke contains harmful chemicals that damage blood vessels, increase blood pressure, and contribute to the development of atherosclerosis. Smoking is a major modifiable risk factor.
